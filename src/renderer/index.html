Production

Prod account setup

Start from the company number. MDM decides whether the account and its TID are already there.

Company

The company number is the TPA. Open TP Admin

MDM credentials

Open the access key in Console, Secrets section. Consumer key is the client id. Consumer Secret 1 is the client secret.

Account TID

Raise a DEVSERVICE ticket to Team Cotton. They create the TTC account. MDM then writes that id as the account TID.

People

Check one person by email or by Trimble Identity id. The result shows whether they are on this company, and the relation when they are.

Subscription

The base64 value the DTD team provides as LEM_SLM_CREDENTIALS. Request this from DTD team.

Optional. Paste the signed-in user's Bearer to check the product.
